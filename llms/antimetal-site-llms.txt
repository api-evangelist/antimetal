# Antimetal

> Antimetal handles everything that happens after you deploy. It investigates, fixes, and prevents production issues by connecting your cloud platforms, observability tools, and code.

Antimetal is an AI-powered platform for software engineering teams. When an alert fires, Antimetal pulls together data from logs, metrics, traces, code, and incident history to identify the root cause — then generates production-ready fixes your team can review, validate, and ship.
